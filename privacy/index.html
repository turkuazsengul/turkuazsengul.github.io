Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what data is processed when you play games published by Cyvana Games (“we”, “us”), currently Glow Gunner X: Turret Survivor on iOS and Android. We keep it short because our games collect very little.

Summary

  • No account or sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and receive no copy of it.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob processes device data to serve and measure ads; see below.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

1. Data stored on your device

Coins, upgrades, settings (sound, language, quality), best scores and the current run are saved locally on your device. This data is deleted when you uninstall the game. It is not sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to show rewarded and interstitial ads. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • the advertising identifier of your device (Google Advertising ID on Android; IDFA on iOS only if you allow tracking),
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • ad interactions (views, clicks).

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent using Google’s consent tool before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Options → Privacy → Ad preferences. On iOS, the app asks for permission to track via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency; if you decline, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised with it. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases (for example “Remove Ads” or coin packs) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. They handle your payment information under their own privacy policies. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so the item can be delivered and restored.

4. Leaderboards (Game Center / Google Play Games)

If you use leaderboards, your best score is submitted to Apple Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games Services (Android) together with the player profile provided by that service. These services are operated by Apple and Google under their own policies. Leaderboards are optional.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use our own analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Apple and Google may provide us aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example installs and crash counts) through their developer consoles.

6. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey’s Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, most requests are fulfilled by the controls above (in-game ad preferences, device advertising settings, uninstalling the game) or by Google/Apple. You can still contact us and we will help.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
